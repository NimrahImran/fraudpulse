Every payment is checked in under a millisecond. Real customers pass straight through, fraud is stopped, and every decision comes with plain reasons.

Real-time scoring

Each payment gets a risk score from 0 to 100 in about 0.1 ms, before money leaves the account.

Pattern detection

Learns five fraud patterns: stolen cards, account takeover, card testing, abroad spikes and late-night bursts.

Explained decisions
